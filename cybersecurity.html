SafeSphere Fundamentals

What is Cybersecurity?

Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting systems, networks, devices, and data from digital attacks, unauthorized access, or damage. It ensures that your personal information, financial transactions, and online identity remain private and secure.

The 3 Core Pillars

🔒

Confidentiality

Keeping sensitive information private and ensuring it is accessible only to authorized people.

🛡️

Integrity

Protecting data against unauthorized changes, tampering, or deletion.

⚡

Availability

Ensuring services, systems, and data are always reliable and available when you need them.

Common Digital Threats

Phishing & Social Engineering

Fake emails, messages, or calls that trick you into revealing sensitive passwords, OTPs, or credit card details.

Malware & Ransomware

Harmful software secretly installed on devices to steal files, spy on activity, or lock systems for ransom.

Identity Theft

Attacks where criminals steal personal credentials to impersonate you, open fake accounts, or make fraud transfers.

💡 Daily Safety Rules

  • Strong Passwords: Never reuse the same password across multiple online accounts.
  • Enable 2FA: Add an extra layer of security to prevent unauthorized logins.
  • Verify Before Clicking: Always double-check web URLs and email senders before clicking links.
