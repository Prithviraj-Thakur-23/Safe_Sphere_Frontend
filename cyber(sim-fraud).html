Cyber Security Threat Advisory

SIM Swap / SIM-Related Fraud

SIM swap fraud occurs when criminals covertly obtain a replacement SIM card for your mobile number without authorization, allowing them to intercept incoming OTPs, calls, and execute financial thefts.

“If your SIM suddenly stops working, don't panic and don't share your OTP. Contact your telecom operator immediately.”

Real-Life Scenario

Sudden Network Loss

You suddenly see “No Network” on your phone and cannot make calls or receive SMS. Simultaneously, a scammer activates a new SIM card using your mobile number to intercept your authentication codes and banking OTPs.

Understanding the Threat

What Is It?

An unauthorized duplicate SIM card issued in your name. Your old SIM loses connection while the scammer’s SIM becomes active on your mobile identity.

How It Works

Criminals harvest your personal data, spoof or manipulate telecom processes to issue a replacement SIM, intercept your OTPs, and access your bank, UPI, email, and social accounts.

Scammer's Psychological Tricks

Fear & False Urgency

Scammers pose as bank or telecom employees, using aggressive scare tactics such as: “Your SIM will be blocked immediately!” or “Complete your KYC verification right now!” Their goal is to induce panic and force compliance.

Red Flags (Clear Warning Signs)

  • Your phone unexpectedly displays “No Network” for an extended period.
  • You are abruptly unable to send or receive calls and text messages.
  • You receive unrequested SMS notifications regarding a SIM replacement.
  • Callers demand your OTP, PIN, password, or sensitive personal credentials.
  • You get unexpected alerts for bank logins or unauthorized financial transactions.
  • High-pressure demands to complete KYC updates or SIM verification immediately.

How to Stay Safe

Guard Your Credentials

Never share your OTP, PIN, password, or personal details with anyone over phone or chat.

Verify Caller Identity

Never trust callers threatening immediate SIM disconnection. Contact your telecom operator solely via official customer-care channels.

Avoid Untrusted Links

Do not click unknown links delivered via SMS or WhatsApp, and maintain strong, unique passwords across accounts.

Emergency Action Steps

1. Contact Operator

Instantly report suspected SIM swap, block the fraudulent card, and restore your number.

2. Financial Help (1930)

If funds were stolen, call 1930 immediately and alert your bank.

3. Report Online & Keep Evidence

File a complaint on cybercrime.gov.in, report fraud messages on Sanchar Saathi (Chakshu), and save SMS/transaction logs.

Official Portals & Emergency Resources:
  • National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal: https://www.cybercrime.gov.in/ (For reporting online financial and cyber fraud)
  • Cyber Crime Helpline: Call 1930 immediately for financial fraud emergencies.
  • Sanchar Saathi – Chakshu: https://sancharsaathi.gov.in/ (To report fraudulent calls, SMS, and WhatsApp messages)
