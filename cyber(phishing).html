🎣 Phishing, Fake Links & Email/SMS Scams

📌 What Is It?

Phishing is a cyber attack where fraudsters send fake messages, emails, or links while pretending to be legitimate organizations such as banks, government bodies, utility providers, or courier companies.

Primary Goal: To deceive you into sharing confidential details like passwords, ATM PINs, CVVs, or personal ID numbers.

📱 Real-Life Fraud Examples

💡 Electricity Bill Disconnection
"Dear Consumer, your electricity power will be disconnected at 9:30 PM tonight because your last bill was not updated. Immediately call officer at 98XXXXXXXX or pay ₹10 via this link to update."
🏦 Bank Account/Card Suspension
"Your SBI / HDFC Bank Net Banking account has been locked due to pending PAN card verification. Click here [fake-bank-portal.xyz] to restore access within 24 hours."
🎁 Lottery / Reward Cashback
"Congratulations! You have received a cashback reward of ₹4,999 on Google Pay / PhonePe. Tap this link to claim your reward straight to your bank account."
📦 Parcel / Courier Delivery Failure
"Your courier package could not be delivered due to an incorrect house address. Click the link to update your details and pay a ₹5 re-delivery fee."

⚙️ How Does It Work?

Step 1
The Hook
The attacker broadcasts mass emails or SMS containing a malicious link paired with an urgent or lucrative statement.
Step 2
The Bait
Clicking the link opens a duplicate site carefully forged to look identical to your bank or provider.
Step 3
Data Capture
You are prompted to enter credentials, bank details, card numbers, or government ID numbers.
Step 4
The Theft
The scammer captures this data in real-time to drain funds, hijack accounts, or commit identity fraud.
🎭 Scammer's Tricks
🚨 Fabricated Urgency: Creating panic (e.g., 'Power cut in 2 hours', 'Account blocked immediately') so you act without thinking.
🚨 Visual Impersonation: Using genuine logos, official-looking fonts, and slightly altered URLs (e.g., www.onlinesbi-update.com instead of www.onlinesbi.sbi).
🚨 Appealing to Greed: Offering unexpected jackpots, instant loan approvals, or tax refunds.
🚩 Red Flags (Warning Signs)
⚠️ Messages from normal 10-digit mobile numbers instead of registered company sender IDs (e.g., AD-HDFCBK or VK-SBIINB).
⚠️ Spelling mistakes, broken grammar, or awkward phrasing.
⚠️ Generic greetings like "Dear Customer" or "Dear User" instead of your name.
⚠️ Shortened or suspicious URLs (e.g., bit.ly, tinyurl, .xyz, .top, .apk).
⚠️ Demands to pay small token fees (₹5 or ₹10) to update profiles or receive parcels.
🛡️ How to Stay Safe
✅ Never Click Unsolicited Links: Avoid links sent via SMS, WhatsApp, or email from unknown senders.
✅ Verify Directly: Open official banking apps or type verified websites directly in your browser.
✅ Check Sender Header: Official providers use enterprise sender headers, never personal numbers.
✅ Enable Security Features: Use spam filters on messaging apps and keep mobile OS updated.
🚨 What to Do If It Happens
⚡ Block Affected Cards & Accounts: Immediately call your bank to freeze net banking and debit/credit cards.
⚡ Change Credentials: Update passwords and PINs immediately using a separate secure device.
⚡ Preserve Evidence: Take screenshots of the SMS, sender number, fraudulent link, and alerts.
⚡ Report Quickly: File a report immediately on the National Cybercrime Reporting Portal.
