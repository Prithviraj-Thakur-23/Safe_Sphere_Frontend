Financial Safety Advisory

Investment Scams

Deceptive fraudulent schemes designed to lure individuals into depositing money into fake investment opportunities, unverified trading applications, or misleading advisory plans.

Real-Life Scenario

The Social Media Trading Group & Fake Profit Trap

A person joins an online group on messaging platforms like WhatsApp or Telegram promising exceptionally high returns. A fraudulent trading app initially displays fake profits to build credibility, but when the victim attempts to withdraw their funds, withdrawals are blocked or extra fees are demanded.

Understanding the Threat

What Is It?

Fraudulent schemes that manipulate individuals into investing funds through fabricated market opportunities, third-party APK apps, or false advisory guidance.

How It Works

Scammers deliberately build trust, showcase fake profit screenshots or fabricated testimonials, encourage larger capital investments, and ultimately lock users out of their accounts.

Common Scammer Disguises & Tactics

Fraudsters entice target investors using manufactured lures:

📈 Guaranteed / High Returns
👤 Fake Financial Experts
🖼️ Fabricated Profit Screenshots
⚡ Pressure to Invest Quickly

The Core Deception

Scammers often permit small initial withdrawals to manufacture false credibility, tricking targets into depositing substantial sums before freezing access.

Red Flags (Clear Warning Signs)

  • Promises of guaranteed, risk-free, or unrealistically high investment returns.
  • Unsolicited investment tips or group invites on social media and chat apps.
  • Prompts to download unknown trading applications via third-party APK links.
  • Instructions to transfer funds into unrelated personal or corporate bank accounts.
  • Sudden demands for withdrawal fees or taxes before releasing profits.

How to Stay Safe

Verify via Regulators (SEBI)

Always verify investment platforms, brokers, and market intermediaries through SEBI or official financial regulatory registers.

Ignore Unsolicited Social Media Tips

Never trust unverified investment advice from social media groups, and avoid unrealistic return promises.

Avoid Third-Party APKs

Never download investment or trading applications via unverified third-party APK links or unknown sources.

Emergency Action Steps

1. Stop Sending Money Immediately

Instantly halt all communications and payments. Preserve complete transaction records, screenshots, and chat logs.

2. Contact Bank & Report Portal

Contact your bank immediately to block further transfers. Report the fraud online at cybercrime.gov.in or call 1930.

3. File SEBI SCORES Complaint

For securities-market grievances, utilize SEBI's official grievance redressal mechanism via the SCORES portal.

Official Sources & Regulatory Guidelines:
  • Regulatory Body: Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) - https://www.sebi.gov.in/
  • SEBI Grievance Redressal (SCORES): https://scores.gov.in/
  • National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal: https://www.cybercrime.gov.in/
  • Cyber Crime Helpline: Call 1930.
