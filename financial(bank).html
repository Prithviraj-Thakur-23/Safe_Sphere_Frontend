Financial Safety Advisory

Fake Loan / KYC / Banking Call Scams

Deceptive telephonic scams where fraudsters impersonate authorized banks, Non-Banking Financial Companies (NBFCs), or loan providers to steal sensitive personal, KYC, and banking credentials.

Real-Life Scenario

The Expired KYC & Pre-Approved Loan Trap

A person receives an unexpected call claiming their bank KYC has expired or that they are pre-approved for an instant low-interest loan. The caller pressures the victim for personal details, identification documents, and banking OTPs to complete loan "processing."

Understanding the Threat

What Is It?

Scams in which malicious callers impersonate legitimate financial institutions to illicitly acquire personal records, KYC documents, and account credentials.

How It Works

Scammers lure targets with instant loan offers or manufacture panic via KYC expiration threats, subsequently harvesting personal data, documents, OTPs, or upfront processing fees.

Common Scammer Disguises & Tactics

Fraudsters manipulate victims using manufactured urgency:

⏱️ Artificial Urgency & Panic
🏦 Fake Bank / NBFC Identity
💸 Attractive Low-Interest Loans
⚠️ Account Suspension Threats

The Core Deception

Scammers exploit fear of account suspension or excitement over easy credit to bypass rational skepticism, tricking targets into revealing confidential authentication codes.

Red Flags (Clear Warning Signs)

  • Urgent messages or calls claiming "KYC expires today" or threatening immediate account blocking.
  • Instant loan offers requiring zero credit verification or background checks.
  • Demands for upfront processing fees, insurance charges, or security deposits prior to loan disbursement.
  • Requests for confidential banking details, including PINs, passwords, or transaction OTPs.
  • Prompts to download unverified remote-access apps or click unknown third-party links.

How to Stay Safe

Use Official Channels Only

Always interact via official bank apps or verified helpline numbers. Legitimate lenders never charge fees prior to loan disbursement via personal accounts.

Never Share OTPs or Install Unknown Apps

Never share your OTP, PIN, or banking passwords with anyone. Avoid installing remote-access applications requested by callers.

Verify Lenders & Contact Directly

Verify lenders on official regulatory registries. For actual KYC updates, visit your bank branch directly or use official banking portals.

Emergency Action Steps

1. Stop Communication & Contact Bank

Immediately disconnect the call, halt communication, and contact your bank or NBFC through official channels to secure your account.

2. Report Unauthorized Transactions

Report any unauthorized deductions to your bank instantly and file a cyber fraud report at cybercrime.gov.in or call 1930.

3. Preserve Evidence & Report Apps

Save caller phone numbers, chat logs, call recordings, and screenshots. Report fraudulent loan apps to law enforcement authorities.

Official Sources & Regulatory Guidelines:
  • Regulatory Body: Reserve Bank of India (RBI).
  • National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal: https://www.cybercrime.gov.in/
  • Cyber Crime Helpline: Call 1930 for immediate financial fraud reporting.
  • Emergency Police Helpline: Call 112.
