Safe Use of AI & Chatbots Guide

AI chatbots are computer systems that can understand prompts and generate responses. They are useful for education, coding, writing, customer service, and many other tasks.

🚨 Real-World Scenario

You receive a WhatsApp voice message that sounds exactly like your friend:
"I'm in an emergency. Please send me ₹20,000 immediately."
The voice may have been created using AI voice-cloning technology.

⚙️ How AI Chatbots Work

1

Input Entry: You enter a question or prompt into the interface.

2

Processing: The AI model processes your input parameters.

3

Generation: The system generates a response, sometimes incorporating tools like web search.

4

Verification: Because AI outputs can occasionally be incorrect or misleading, important facts should always be verified.

🎭 AI Scammer Tricks

  • AI-generated fake voice cloning.
  • Deceptive fake AI videos and images (deepfakes).
  • Fake AI customer-support accounts.
  • Fraudulent fake AI investment schemes.
  • Sophisticated AI-generated phishing messages.
  • Fake AI websites and fraudulent apps.
  • Coercing users into uploading sensitive documents.

🚩 Major Red Flags

  • Someone urgently demands money using an AI-generated voice or video.
  • A website or service promises "guaranteed profit with AI."
  • An AI service requests unnecessary sensitive information.
  • A chatbot asks for passwords, OTPs, or banking information.
  • A fake AI app requests permission to install unknown software.

🛡️ Prevention & Safety Rules

  • Never share OTPs, PINs, or passwords with a chatbot.
  • Avoid uploading sensitive documents unnecessarily.
  • Always verify important AI-generated information.
  • Verify unusual money requests through an alternate communication method.
  • Use official websites and apps for financial transactions.
  • Never trust a voice or video alone as absolute proof of identity.

⚡ Incident Response

  • Never share passwords, OTPs, PINs, or banking details.
  • Verify suspicious voice or video requests using another communication method.
  • Never send money based solely on a voice or video message.
  • Stick strictly to official websites and verified apps.
  • Cross-check and verify important AI-generated facts.
  • Report fraudulent accounts and malicious content immediately.
  • If money is lost, contact your bank and report the incident right away.
