Identity & Financial Access Fraud

OTP Scams & Mitigation Dashboard

An OTP (One-Time Password) scam occurs when a scammer manipulates a victim into disclosing their single-use security code, completely bypassing Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) to siphon funds or hijack digital accounts.

🚨 Real-Life Scammer Tactics & Scenarios

🔄 Accidental Money Transfer

"Sir, I mistakenly transferred ₹10,000 to your PhonePe account. Please read out the 6-digit cancellation OTP so the money goes back."

📦 Delivery Package Confirmation

"I am at your gate with your delivery, but the system is locked. Give me the verification code you just received to confirm delivery."

📶 Free SIM 5G Upgrade

"Calling from telecom support to upgrade your SIM to 5G instantly. Please share the OTP sent by the company to activate your request."

💳 Credit Card Rewards

"Your reward points worth ₹8,000 will expire tonight. Please share the verification OTP to convert them straight into cash."

⚙️ How Does the Scam Work?

1

Target Acquisition: Attacker acquires your phone number, card, or username.

2

Initiating Action: Attacker triggers a transfer, purchase, or password reset request.

3

OTP Generation: Bank sends a secure transaction code to your mobile device.

4

Social Engineering: Attacker calls concurrently, tricking you into reading code aloud.

🎭 Scammer Mind Tricks

  • Disguising Terminology: Referring to the OTP as a "cancellation code", "security token", or "refund pin".
  • Continuous Engagement: Keeping you occupied on the active call so you fail to read the SMS text showing the actual debited amount.
  • Authority & Urgency: Posing as bank managers, fraud squad officers, or telecom technicians creating false panic.

🚩 Major Red Flags

  • Anyone asking you to read out an SMS code or verification number over call or chat.
  • Receiving unexpected OTPs for transactions or password resets you never started.
  • SMS body containing phrases like "Debited for INR..." while the caller claims it is to receive money.
  • Extreme pressure to share the code before it "expires in 30 seconds".

🛡️ Defense & Safety Rules

  • Strict No-Sharing Policy: Treat your OTP like your ATM PIN—never share it with anyone under any circumstance.
  • Read the Full SMS: Check the purpose, merchant name, and exact amount in the text before typing anywhere.
  • The Golden Rule of UPI: You never need to enter an OTP or PIN to receive money into your account.
  • Disconnect Instantly: Hang up immediately if someone asks for a code, regardless of their stated reason.
