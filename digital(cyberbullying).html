Social Media Safety & Online Harassment

Cyberbullying and Online Harassment Guide

Cyberbullying is the use of digital platforms to repeatedly insult, threaten, embarrass, intimidate, or harass another person through social media, messaging apps, email, online games, and other platforms.

🚨 Real-Life Examples

  • Someone creates a fake Instagram account using another student's name and photo.
  • A person repeatedly sends abusive messages on WhatsApp.
  • Someone shares a person's edited or morphed photo without permission.
  • A student is repeatedly insulted in a college WhatsApp group.
  • Someone threatens to share a person's private photos or information.
  • A group of people repeatedly leaves hateful comments on someone's social media posts.

⚙️ How It Works

  • Harassers may create fake social media accounts using your name or photograph.
  • They send abusive or threatening messages through WhatsApp, Instagram, or other apps.
  • They share private information, photographs, or videos without permission.
  • They repeatedly contact you even after you block or ask them to stop.
  • The harassment can persist by shifting to new accounts or different platforms.

🎭 Harasser's Tricks

  • Hiding behind fake accounts or anonymous profiles.
  • Using edited or manipulated photographs to intimidate victims.
  • Sending coordinated threatening messages.
  • Engaging in group harassment or targeted impersonation.

🚩 Major Red Flags

  • Repeated abusive messages from known or unknown profiles.
  • Fake accounts operating using your name or photograph.
  • Direct threatening messages or intimidation attempts.
  • Unwanted sharing of private information or media.
  • Edited or manipulated photographs surfacing online.
  • Repeated unwanted contact after being blocked.
  • A coordinated group of people targeting one person online.

🛡️ How to Stay Safe

  • Keep social media accounts set to private when appropriate.
  • Use strong passwords and enable two-factor authentication (2FA).
  • Avoid sharing sensitive personal information publicly online.
  • Block and report abusive accounts immediately.
  • Keep digital evidence of serious harassment (screenshots, links).

⚡ What to Do If It Happens

  • Do not respond with threats or abusive language.
  • Take screenshots and save messages, usernames, and URLs.
  • Block and report the offending accounts.
  • Inform a trusted parent, teacher, college authority, or responsible person.
  • Report serious cyber harassment through the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal.
