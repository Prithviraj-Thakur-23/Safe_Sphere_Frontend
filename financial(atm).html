Financial Safety Advisory

ATM Skimming / Card Trapping

Physical card fraud involving tampered ATMs equipped with unauthorized hardware designed to steal card data, capture PIN codes, or mechanically trap cards inside the terminal.

Real-Life Scenario

The Tampered Slot, Trapped Card & Hidden Camera Trap

A user visits a compromised ATM where an unauthorized skimmer device captures magnetic stripe or chip data, while a mechanical trap prevents the card from being returned normally. Hidden micro-cameras or false overlays capture the user entering their confidential PIN.

Understanding the Threat

What Is It?

A type of physical card fraud utilizing tampered ATM hardware components to capture card credentials or physically interfere with transaction completion.

How It Works

Fraudsters install malicious attachments over card readers and keypads or insert mechanical blockers inside the slot to intercept card data and trap physical cards during usage.

Common Scammer Tactics & Assistance Traps

Perpetrators rely on concealed equipment and social engineering:

👁️ Hidden Micro-Cameras
🛑 Mechanical Card Trapping
🤝 Phony Bystander "Assistance"
🔧 Tampered Keypad Overlays

The Helpful Bystander Deception

When your card gets unexpectedly stuck or trapped in the machine, fraudsters standing nearby pose as helpful assistants to guide you to re-enter your PIN while they observe or manipulate the terminal.

Red Flags (Clear Warning Signs)

  • Loose, bulky, or unusual card insertion slots with glue residue or mismatched plastic faceplates.
  • Suspicious devices, attachments, or pinhole cameras mounted above or around the ATM bezel.
  • Unusual or unresponsive keypads that feel unusually thick or rigid.
  • Your physical card failing to return normally at the end of a transaction.
  • Unexpected transaction alerts for withdrawals you did not authorize.

How to Stay Safe

Inspect ATMs Before Use

Inspect the card slot and keypad for loose parts, unusual attachments, or glue residue. Use secure, well-lit ATMs located inside bank branches.

Shield Your PIN & Enable Alerts

Always cover the keypad with your free hand while entering your PIN. Keep instant SMS and app transaction alerts active at all times.

Never Accept Help from Strangers

Never accept assistance or advice from strangers standing near an ATM if your card gets stuck or trapped.

Emergency Action Steps

1. Stay by Machine & Block Card

Stay by the machine if safe while calling your bank hotline immediately to block the compromised card and prevent unauthorized withdrawals.

2. Inform Bank & Operator

Notify the ATM operator or bank about suspicious equipment, skimming devices, or physical card trapping.

3. Report Cyber Fraud Portal

Report financial cyber fraud immediately on the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal at cybercrime.gov.in or call helpline 1930.

Official Sources & Regulatory Guidelines:
  • Regulatory Bodies: Reserve Bank of India (RBI) & National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI).
  • National Cyber Crime Helpline: Call 1930 for reporting financial cyber fraud.
  • National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal: https://www.cybercrime.gov.in/
  • Emergency Police Helpline: Call 112.
