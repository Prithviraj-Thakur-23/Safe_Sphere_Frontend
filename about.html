SafeSphere

A Unified Platform for Digital, Financial and Personal Safety

SafeSphere is a safety awareness platform created to provide simple, practical and accessible safety information in one place. Our goal is to help people understand different risks, identify warning signs and know the right steps to take when they face a safety-related situation.

🛡️ About Our Project

SafeSphere was developed as a Community Engagement Project to spread awareness about digital, financial, personal and community safety.

The platform brings safety awareness resources, information about common scams and threats, emergency contacts, reporting guidance and useful awareness content together in one user-friendly website.

Our focus is not only on explaining a problem, but also on helping users understand what they can do to prevent it and where they can seek help.
