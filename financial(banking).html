Financial Safety Advisory

Banking Fraud

Deceptive schemes where scammers impersonate bank representatives or officials to extract confidential banking credentials, OTPs, or unauthorized money transfers.

Real-Life Scenario

The Bank Account Block Threat & Remote Access Trap

Someone receives a call claiming to be from their bank and is told that their account will be blocked unless they provide an OTP and account details. In other dangerous cases, scammers trick users into downloading remote-access screensharing software (such as AnyDesk or TeamViewer) under the guise of customer support.

Understanding the Threat

What Is It?

Fraud where scammers impersonate legitimate bank authorities or officials to obtain confidential banking credentials, login details, or funds.

How It Works

The scammer contacts the victim through phone calls, SMS, email, or social media channels, using high-pressure tactics to harvest sensitive information or compel the installation of unauthorized apps.

Common Scammer Disguises & Tactics

Fraudsters manipulate victims by manufacturing panic using official-sounding pretexts:

🚨 "Account Blocked" Threats
📄 "KYC Expired" Notices
⚠️ "Suspicious Transaction" Alerts
📞 Spoofed Official Caller IDs

The Core Deception

Scammers use spoofed phone numbers to look genuine and pressure targets into acting instantly without hanging up or verifying details.

Red Flags (Clear Warning Signs)

  • Caller asks for confidential OTP, PIN, or passwords.
  • Requests to download unknown or remote-access applications.
  • Urgent threats of account closure, suspension, or freezing.
  • Suspicious links sent via SMS or email claiming to be from your bank.
  • Callers insisting you stay on the line and act immediately without hanging up.

How to Stay Safe

Never Share Sensitive Credentials

Never share your OTP, PIN, password, CVV, or banking login details with anyone under any circumstances.

Verify via Official Channels

Always contact your bank through its official website, mobile application, or verified known customer-care numbers.

Reject Remote Desktop Apps

Never install remote desktop or screen-sharing apps like AnyDesk or TeamViewer upon a caller's request.

Emergency Action Steps

1. Contact Your Bank Instantly

Immediately notify your bank to freeze accounts, block affected banking instruments, and report unauthorized transactions.

2. Preserve Evidence

Keep detailed logs of the communication, call recordings, SMS texts, and screenshots for investigation.

3. File Formal Dispute & Report

File a formal dispute with your bank and report the fraud immediately at cybercrime.gov.in or call 1930.

Official Sources & Regulatory Guidelines:
  • Regulatory Body: Reserve Bank of India (RBI).
  • National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal: https://www.cybercrime.gov.in/
  • Cyber Crime Helpline: Call 1930 for immediate financial fraud reporting.
  • Emergency Police Helpline: Call 112.
