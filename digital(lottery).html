Lottery & Prize Scams Guide

A lottery or prize scam is a fraud in which scammers falsely claim that a person has won money, a gift, a vehicle, or another valuable prize, tricking victims into paying money or sharing personal information.

🚨 Real-Life Examples

  • You receive a message saying you have won ₹10 lakh in a lottery you never entered.
  • Someone calls claiming you won a car but asks for ₹5,000 as a processing fee.
  • A WhatsApp message says you won an iPhone and asks you to pay delivery charges.
  • A fake representative asks for your OTP and bank details to transfer your prize.
  • An email says you won an international lottery and must pay tax first.
  • A scammer sends a fake government letter claiming a reward.

⚙️ How It Works

  • The scammer contacts you via call or message claiming you won a prize.
  • They request sensitive details like your OTP, bank details, or UPI PIN.
  • They demand advance payment for taxes, processing fees, or registration charges.
  • If you make the payment, they demand more money or stop responding entirely.
  • The promised prize is never delivered under any circumstances.

🎭 Scammer Tricks

  • Use of fake lottery messages and fabricated certificates.
  • Displaying forged company logos and fraudulent websites.
  • Sending high-pressure urgent messages.
  • Pretending to represent well-known corporations or government organizations.

🚩 Major Red Flags

  • You never entered the lottery, contest, or sweepstakes.
  • You are promised a very large prize unexpectedly.
  • You are asked to pay money upfront before receiving the prize.
  • The message contains a suspicious or shortened link.
  • The caller asks for OTP, PIN, CVV, or bank details.
  • The scammer creates urgency saying the prize expires soon.

🛡️ How to Stay Safe

  • Never pay an advance fee to claim an unexpected prize.
  • Do not share OTP, PIN, CVV, passwords, or banking information.
  • Verify the offer through the organization's official website.
  • Avoid clicking unknown or shortened links.
  • CERT-In Guidance: Never pay an advance fee to claim a competition prize.

⚡ What to Do If It Happens

  • Stop communicating with the scammer and make no further payments.
  • Save screenshots, phone numbers, messages, and transaction records.
  • If money was lost, immediately contact your bank and report the incident via the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal.
