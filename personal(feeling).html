Personal Safety

Feeling Threatened or Followed in a Public Place

👣

Real-Life Example & Immediate Actions

  • Notice if the same stranger repeatedly appears behind you.
  • Change direction and enter a busy shop or safe public place.
  • Tell staff, call a trusted family member, and ask for help.
  • Do not go straight home if you think you are being followed.
🔍 What Is It?
  • It is a situation where another person's behaviour makes you fear for your safety.
  • Examples include repeated following, threats, blocking your path, or forcing unwanted contact.
  • Trust your instincts and move toward help before the situation becomes more dangerous.
⚙️ How Does It Work?
  • A person may copy your route, wait near places you visit, or try to isolate you.
  • Break the pattern by moving to a busy, well-lit place with staff, security, or other people.
🎭 Common Perpetrator Tactics
  • Pretending to be friendly or asking for directions to lower your guard.
  • Claiming to know your family or creating a false emergency.
  • Using emotional pressure to make you enter a vehicle or go to a quiet place.
🚩 Red Flags (Clear Warning Signs)
  • The same person appears after you change direction or cross the street.
  • Someone matches your pace, blocks your route, or watches you closely.
  • Unwanted touching, verbal threats, or attempts to force you into a vehicle.

🛡️ How to Stay Safe

1

Stay Alert & Aware

Stay aware of your surroundings and avoid complete distraction by phones or headphones.

2

Head to Safe Public Zones

Go to a busy shop, security desk, police station, or other safe public place immediately.

3

Share Your Live Location

Call a trusted person, explain the situation clearly, and share your live location.

4

Avoid Direct Confrontation

Do not confront the person directly and never enter an unknown vehicle.

🚨 What to Do If It Happens

1

Alert Local Staff

Tell staff, security personnel, or police that you feel threatened or followed.

2

Call Emergency Services

Dial emergency helplines immediately if you feel in immediate danger.

3

Record Key Details

Record the time, location, physical description, and vehicle details when safe to do so.

4

Report Repeated Incidents

Report repeated suspicious behaviour or stalkers to law enforcement authorities.

📞

In Danger or Feeling Unsafe Right Now?

Call emergency response or women's helpline services immediately for help.

📞 112 – Emergency / Police 📞 1091 – Women Helpline 📞 108 – Ambulance
