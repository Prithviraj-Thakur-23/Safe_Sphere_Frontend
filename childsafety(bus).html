School and School Bus Safety

🚌

Real-Life Example

A student gets off a school bus and immediately runs across the road without checking for traffic. This can create a dangerous situation and increase the risk of an accident.

🏫 What Is It?

School safety means following rules and practices that help protect students while they are at school.

School bus safety includes the rules students should follow while waiting for, boarding, travelling on, and getting off a school bus.

🤝 How Does It Work?

School safety works through close cooperation between students, teachers, parents, and school authorities.

School bus safety depends on responsible driving, proper adult supervision, and students diligently following safety rules.

🕵️ Unsafe Situations & Stranger Tactics

There may not be a traditional scammer, but unsafe situations can involve:

  • A stranger pretending to be school staff or an official worker.
  • Someone falsely claiming that the child's parents sent them to pick them up.
  • Someone asking a child to enter an unauthorized or private vehicle.
  • An unknown person trying to take a child away from the school premises.
🚩 Red Flags (Clear Warning Signs)
  • An unfamiliar person claims to be connected with the school without proper ID.
  • An unauthorized vehicle asks students to get inside.
  • Someone asks a child to leave school without informing parents or teachers.
  • Dangerous or disruptive behaviour happening inside the school bus.
  • Students standing up or moving around while the bus is actively in motion.

🛡️️ How to Stay Safe

1

Authorized Transport Only

Use only officially approved school transport or designated vehicles arranged by parents.

2

Wait for Full Stop

Wait until the school bus comes to a complete stop before boarding or getting off.

3

Remain Seated

Stay properly seated while the bus is in motion to prevent falls or sudden injuries.

4

Do Not Distract Driver

Keep noise levels low and avoid disturbing the driver while they are driving.

5

No Pushing or Running

Never push, shove, or run near the bus or near active traffic zones.

6

Follow Road Rules

Always look both ways before crossing roads and follow basic traffic signals.

7

Verify Unknown Persons

Never leave with an unknown person without direct confirmation from parents or school staff.

🚨 What to Do If It Happens

1

Inform Authorities Instantly

Report any issue or hazard to the bus driver, teacher, or school administrator right away.

2

Move to a Safe Spot

Step away from dangerous traffic, moving vehicles, or suspicious individuals immediately.

3

Contact Parents

Reach out to parents or legal guardians to inform them about the incident or safety concern.

4

Report Suspicious Activity

Tell a trusted adult, school guard, or police official about any stranger acting suspiciously.

5

Call Emergency Services

In case of an accident or immediate danger, call official emergency response services right away.

📞

Need Immediate Emergency Assistance?

Contact child safety services or emergency response numbers for urgent support.
