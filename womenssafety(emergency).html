Women's Emergency & Legal Protection

🆘

Real-Life Example

A woman experiencing domestic abuse or trapped in an unsafe environment needs immediate police intervention and legal counseling but doesn't know where to seek confidential aid.

🏛️ What Is It?

Institutional frameworks, national helplines, emergency apps, and crisis cells designed to protect women facing violence, abuse, or distress.

⚡ How Does It Work?

Round-the-clock emergency numbers connect victims directly to specialized police units, legal advisors, medical help, and temporary shelters.

🎭 Scammer's Trick

Fake support helplines or unauthorized "assistance centers" asking for upfront fees or personal sensitive documents under the guise of offering legal aid.

🚩 Red Flags to Watch For
  • Unverified emergency numbers circulated on social media platforms.
  • Demands for banking credentials or upfront payment for emergency aid.
  • Complete lack of official government affiliation or verified accreditation.

🛡️ How to Stay Safe

1

Speed Dial Numbers

Save official national emergency numbers (e.g., 112, 1091) on speed dial for immediate one-touch access.

2

Official SOS Apps

Download government-verified emergency and safety SOS applications on your smartphone.

3

Share Live Location

Regularly share your real-time location with trusted family members or friends when traveling alone.

4

Verify Channels

Ensure any helpline or shelter center you contact is affiliated with official government bodies.

🚨 What to Do If It Happens

1

Trigger Smartphone SOS

Activate built-in hardware SOS features on your phone to alert emergency services and emergency contacts.

2

Dial Official Helplines

Contact national emergency helplines immediately for urgent response and legal guidance.

3

Reach Sakhi Centres

Seek help from One Stop Centres (Sakhi Centres) for integrated shelter, medical, legal, and psychological aid.

4

Contact Police Station

Approach the nearest police station or specialized Women's Helpdesk to file an official report.

📞

In Immediate Distress or Need Emergency Intervention?

Call government-verified emergency helplines directly for 24/7 confidential support.

📞 Emergency: 112 📞 Women Helpline: 1091 📞 Women Distress: 181
