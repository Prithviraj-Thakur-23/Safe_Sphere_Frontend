How to Use SafeSphere

Your Complete Guide to Navigating Our Safety Tools

SafeSphere is packed with interactive tools, AI-powered scanners, and critical emergency resources. Use this guide to understand exactly how each feature works and how to get the most out of our platform.

🛡️ 1. The AI Threat Scanner (Home Page)

Located on the Home page, our flagship feature uses backend neural analyzers and forensic inspection to protect you from digital threats.

  • Email Phishing & Forensics: Paste suspicious email text or raw `.eml` headers. The engine parses SPF, DKIM, and DMARC alignments, extracts the originating public IP address, and queries geolocation databases to detect VPN or proxy masking.
  • Image/Video Deepfake Tab: Upload an image or video to analyze compression artifacts and generative neural noise indicative of synthetic generation.
  • AI Voice Clone Tab: Upload audio clips to scan acoustic spectra for vocoder anomalies and voice synthesis signatures.

🚨 2. Emergency Contacts & Official Portals

When you are in immediate danger or need to report a crime, SafeSphere provides instant routing to verified official services.

  • One-Tap Calling: The Emergency Contacts card lists primary Indian helplines (1930 for Cyber Crime, 112 for Police, 1091 for Women, 1098 for Child Helpline). Tapping the 📞 icon immediately triggers dialing on mobile devices.
  • Register Complaints: Click any official portal card (Cyber Crime, NCW, PENCIL, Consumer Helpline) to review procedural steps before navigating to the government portal.

📰 3. Live Daily Crime Alerts

The right sidebar features a time-calculated feed providing case studies of recent incidents caused by safety lapses.

  • Automatic Expiration: Incidents are actively measured against system time and purged after 72 hours.
  • Direct References: Every card provides verified publication citations and external links to read complete advisories.

📝 4. Quizzes & Feedback

Interactive education and verified user reviews maintain community accountability.

  • AI Dynamic Quizzes: Selecting any topic automatically prompts Google Gemini to write 10 fresh, practical multiple-choice questions on demand.
  • Community Feedback: Users can post feedback, with an automated check blocking disposable email providers.

📚 5. Safety Knowledge Categories

Access the sidebar (☰) to study reference guidance covering six primary categories:

  • Cyber & Digital Safety: Device hardening, 2FA protocols, password managers, and data breach prevention.
  • Financial Safety: Spotting fake banking portals, avoiding UPI request scams, and ATM skimmer detection.
  • Personal & Women's Safety: Situational awareness habits, Zero FIR procedures, and SOS smartphone features.
  • Child Safety: Parental controls, screen-time regulation, and reporting cyberbullying.

🌐 6. Accessibility Tools

SafeSphere accommodates multilingual access and viewing preferences across all devices.

  • Language Translation: Use the language dropdown (🌐 ENG) to switch entire pages to Hindi or Marathi while keeping the SafeSphere brand intact.
  • Theme Switcher: Switch between Emerald Green, Dark Mode, and Royal Purple. Choices persist across your session.
