Personal Safety & Emergency Response Guide

Comprehensive protocols, preventive strategies, and quick action steps for physical safety in public and emergency environments.

01. Foundation

Core Pillars of Personal Safety

Key proactive behaviors to recognize vulnerabilities early and prevent safety risks.

👀 Situational Awareness

Stay aware of your surroundings by minimizing distractions like looking at phones or using noise-canceling headphones in isolated zones.

🚶‍♀️ Body Posture

Walk with confidence, purpose, and chin up. Perpetrators often target individuals who appear distracted or anxious.

🛡️ Trust Instincts

If a person, alley, or scenario feels unsafe, leave immediately without hesitating or worrying about appearing rude.

📱 Digital Preparation

Keep SOS speed dials active, share live location links with loved ones, and know local emergency helpline numbers.

02. Threat Escalation

Feeling Threatened or Followed in Public

Step-by-step evasive actions to take when you suspect someone is tailing or targeting you.

1
Verify Suspicion

Cross the street purposefully or turn into a side store. If they mirror your path, confirm you are being followed.

2
Head to Busy Spaces

Move toward crowded areas—restaurants, open shops, hotel lobbies, or petrol pumps. Never go directly home.

3
Create Public Distraction

Use a loud, clear voice: "STAY BACK!" Draw public attention instantly to deter the perpetrator.

4
Alert First Responders

Notify venue security or call national helpline 112 / women safety helplines immediately for support.

03. Transit Safety

Public & Shared Transport Safety

Precautionary measures when commuting in cabs, buses, auto-rickshaws, and trains.

🚕 Ride Verification

Check the driver profile, photo, and number plate match your app details before entering any ride-share vehicle.

📍 Live Location Link

Share your real-time travel status via GPS apps with trusted contacts whenever traveling late or in unfamiliar areas.

🚌 Strategic Position

On buses or trains, sit close to the driver, conductor, or in well-lit designated women's compartments.

🎒 Bag & Valuables Care

Keep bag zips closed and wear backpacks on your front in crowded transit platforms to prevent pickpocketing.

04. Night Travel

Walking & Travelling Alone at Night

Safety strategies for late-night commutes and unlit streets.

1
Stick to Well-Lit Main Roads

Avoid dark shortcuts, unlit parks, or isolated vacant plots even if they shorten travel duration.

2
Keep Hands Free & Ready

Avoid overburdening yourself with multiple bags. Keep keys or safety tools accessible in hand.

3
Keep Ears Uncovered

Keep at least one ear free when using earphones so you can hear oncoming footsteps or vehicles.

05. Dense Gatherings

Crowd & Public Event Safety

Preventing injury during festival surges, sports events, and heavy public crowds.

🚪 Identify Emergency Exits

Upon entering any venue or stadium, locate at least two emergency exit points right away.

🌊 Move Diagonally with Surge

If caught in a dense crowd surge, move diagonally across the flow rather than fighting straight against it.

🫁 Protect Your Chest

Hold your arms in front of your chest like a boxer to create breathing space and guard your ribcage.

06. Building Hazards

Fire & Building Emergency Safety

Evacuation procedures during building fires or structural alerts.

1
Stay Low Under Smoke

Toxic smoke rises to the ceiling. Stay close to the floor where air is cooler and cleaner to breathe.

2
Always Use Stairwells

Never take elevators during fire alarms as power cuts can trap you between floors.

3
Test Door Temperature

Touch closed doors with the back of your hand. If hot, do not open—seek an alternate escape route.

07. First Response

Accident & Sudden Emergency Response

Immediate actions during road accidents or medical emergencies.

⚠️ Assess Scene Safety

Ensure the surroundings are safe before assisting victims. Do not endanger yourself on active roadways.

📞 Contact Emergency Services

Call national emergency number 112 or local emergency services immediately. Provide exact landmarks, address, and condition of victims.

🩹 Control Heavy Bleeding

Apply firm, direct pressure on heavy wounds using a clean cloth until professional paramedics arrive.

🚨

In immediate danger? Call National Emergency Response 112 or Women Helpline 1091 instantly.
